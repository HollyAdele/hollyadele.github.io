Vivek Ramaswamy's website says he wants to "slash" property taxes and eliminate income tax. I'm curious where he will get money to govern the state. I like it when governments have money to help the people who need it. I like when the state builds things like roads and libraries and schools. I think life would be worse without public investment. Are there people mooching off the state? I think the answer will depend on who you are asking. If you're accepting public assisstance, you won't call it mooching. If you're funding the public assisstance, you will call it mooching. 

A question I would prefer to ask is, WHY are people using public assistance? Was their education inadequate? (that's a state issue) Is it possible for them to be successful in the economy? (that's a state issue)

Humans have been successful as a species because we worked together. Tribes that cooperated, survived better. Now that we're in cities and civilizations, working together looks like taxes and public spending. 

Humans have natural variation. The circumstances we find ourselves in determines which traits are favorable and unfavorable. For example, it is favorable to have long femurs. Tall people make more money. I want to live in a society where you can have a good life whether or not your traits happen to be favorable. Our society is so abundant that we can support our neighbors who haven't profited quite so much from globalization and automation. 

Globalization (working together around the whole world) and automation (building machines to do work for us) have amplified some traits and not others. The gap between rich and poor has become so big. Some people have millions, billions, and now a trillion more dollars than other people. I'm not sure that the people who have this much money are more deserving of life than those who have less. 

All of this is to say that, as our society progresses, gaps between rich or poor will be amplified more and more. It is ironic that the hardest-working people, and the people with the least pleasant jobs, are paid the least. This is not a stable society. If the gap between poor and rich, workers and leisurers, becomes too big, the people at the bottom will revolt. When they have nothing in society to lose, they will cause everyone to lose.

So I think society should support those who need it. With taxes. 

Will everyone become moochers if that is a valid way of life? I don't think so, because careers bring satisfaction and purpose. It doesn't feel good to scroll on phones all day. 

All of this is to say, I'm uncomfortable with Vivek Ramaswamy's plan to "slash" taxes.
