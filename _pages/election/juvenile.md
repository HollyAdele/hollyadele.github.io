### Nicholas Sylvain
### vs. Hope Smalls

I have heard Hope Smalls speak twice, and she is so inspiring. So, so, inspiring. She has a soft spot in her heart for youth who are struggling. She sees them as humans, as youths who need understanding and guidance and help. She is exactly the person I want my child to see, if they ever end up in the justice system. She speaks about true justice, and evidence-based restoration, and accountability. I have read too many books about "justice," where a person is handed a punishment that makes the problem worse, not better. 

Nicholas Sylvain received his Chemical Engineering degree from UD. He was appointed to this position. He said that it is exciting that he will leverage his years of experience to help children and families find justice and improve their lives. He's probably a fine judge, but I believe lasting peace will come through the work of Hope Smalls.
