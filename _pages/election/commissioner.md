### Carolyn Rice
### vs Margie Christie

Margie Christie used to be a travel agent. When the internet automated that business, she became the director of Dayton Right to Life. 

Carolyn Rice 
 - provided stable, long-term housing to 862 households in 2025, including 161 with children.
 - provided temporary housing to 748 homeless households
 - transferred 70 properties back into active use, sparking $100 million in private investment
 - administers 143.7 million annually in local safety-net spending

 Margie Christie runs on a platform of spending less. Being "responsible" with tax dollars. Taking in less tax dollars. 

 I think this strategy will increase suffering in our county. I think it will increase homelessness. I think it will increase abortions. 

 I don't like abortions either. I want to decrease them. Margie Christie and I have the same goal. No aborted babies. 

 I just think that making abortions illegal is not the most effective route. Instead, we have women dying of sepsis in hospital parking lots because doctors won't perform the life-saving abortion. This is not respectful of Life. Those women do not have a Right to Life when abortions are illegal. 

 The thing proven to reduce abortions is ready access to contraception. Another thing that women said may have helped them to not have an abortion is economic support and better housing. These are things that conservatives want to "save money" on. 

 County commissioner oversees 4300 employees and 1.2 billion dollars. Disability services, water, trash, courts, jails, work programs, preschool promise, and career preparation. I don't want to skimp on these priorities. I want community members to be supported in their needs. I want my neighbors to be supported in their hardships. Carolyn Rice is already doing this. Disrupting her work will have a high cost to the most vulnerable in our community. Even if I thought Margie Christie would deliver the same level of aid to my neighbors (which she already said she wouldn't), there would be many months or years of learning before she could perform at capacity.
