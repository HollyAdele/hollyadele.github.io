### Kelly Madzey 
### vs Michele Henne

Madzey graduated "summa cum laude" in criminal justice and juris doctor "with distinction". So that's cool. She got a letter of commendation from the Dayton Police Department for choosing to go above and beyond her requirements to keep citizens safe. And a separate certificate of appreciation a year or so later. 

Henne was appointed by Mike DeWine to serve in the position she is running for reeelection for. 

They are/were both prosecutors, about the same age, with the same amount of online respectability. Henne was appointed by a Republican governor, Madzey has a lot of education and passion related to criminal justice. Madzey has received several awards for her difficult, extra work related to criminal justice. 

Henne was a keynote speaker at an addiction center opening and at UD's graduation. She was likely asked because she was a judge, and she was a judge possibly because she was conservative and appointed by a conservative governor. Madzey was endorsed by police, who usually only endorse Republican candidates. But nevertheless Henne has more experience. This race was on the fence for me, but I'm tilting toward Henne. Either choice would elect a good judge, I feel.
