### Kelly Madzey 
### vs Michele Henne

Madzey graduated "summa cum laude" in criminal justice and juris doctor "with distinction". So that's cool. She got a letter of commendation from the Dayton Police Department for choosing to go above and beyond her requirements to keep citizens safe. And a separate certificate of appreciation a year or so later. 

Henne was appointed by Mike DeWine to serve in the position she is running for reeelection for. 

They are/were both prosecutors, about the same age, with the same amount of online respectability. Henne was appointed by a Republican governor, Madzey has a lot of education and passion related to criminal justice. Madzey has received several awards for her difficult, extra work related to criminal justice. 

Henne was a keynote speaker at an addiction center opening and at UD's graduation. She was likely asked because she was a judge, and she was a judge possibly because she was conservative and appointed by a conservative governor. Madzey was endorsed by police, who usually only endorse Republican candidates. But nevertheless Henne has more experience. This race was on the fence for me, but I'm tilting toward Henne. This is a gut feeling. Either choice would elect a good judge, I feel. 

The chair of the Montgomery County Democrat Party spoke with Madzey and said she was very thoughtful. She said Madzey would bring a lot of thought to the bench. So that's good. 

I reached out to Judge Henne (rhymes with Penny) on Facebook. She said as a judge, she's not allowed to endorse or condemn a political party. She had a very fair and balanced repsonse back to me. When I asked her if she approached the Republicans for endorsement, or if they reached out to her first, she didn't answer immediately. I'll update this if she answers. 

To be clear, Madzey is the Democrat and Henne is the Republican. They're "non-partisan". Obviously. 

I haven't settled this in my mind yet. You can't get elected without a party, but if you're in a party you have to support all the same things as your party, which are only loosely related. If you've got clarity on this, text me at the number that was on the flyer.
