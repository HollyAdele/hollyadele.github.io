### Colleen O'Donnell
### vs. Jennifer Brunner

Colleen O'Donnell was an immigration judge at the Texas border. The fourth sentence on her campaign website said that she never once granted asylum. 

That's a hard no for me. The United States birthrate is going down, and is now below replacement level. Our population will age and shrink. When cities shrink, buildings shutter closed and fall into disrepair. Fewer workers have to support a greater number of elderly. There is less capitol for business inestment. The economy shrinks. Investments don't grow.

I think we need immigration. Yet Colleen O'Donnell let zero immigrants in who were fleeing dangerous situations. I don't know what it's like to be the person to hear these harrowing tales, say no, and send the person back into peril. 

She admitted not one person?

During the Holocaust, a ship of hundreds of Jews docked at a US harbor and asked for asylum. The United States said no, and sent the ship back. Most of those Jews died violent deaths in Europe. 

Since then, the United States has believed in taking in the suffering. I believe in taking in the suffering. I thought that was a part of Christianity. I see a lot of Christian words in the Trump administration. I expect to see a lot of taking in the stranger, helping the poor and needy, and being honest. (But I don't.)

I would like justices on the Supreme Court in my state to have compassion for the suffering. 

Jennifer Brunner has decades of experience. She was the one who started Ohio's drug court and our mental health court. 

I think we need a justice who is involved in fixing the drug crisis, and not one who is focused on keeping outsiders out.
