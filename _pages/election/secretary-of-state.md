### Tom Pruss
### vs Allison Russo
### vs Robert Sprague

Pruss: His website looks good, but I haven't heard of him. If I haven't heard of him, the majority of Ohioans haven't either, and he will just split the anti-corruption vote.   
Russo: very much for fair elections. gobs of experience in policy.  opposed HB 6.
Sprague: some good policies, but stands for investment without regard to ethics. gave non-public voter information to Trump's Department of "Justice". 

Sprague received donations from the PACs involved in the Ohio Nuclear Bribery Scandal, but not very many, and he wasn't convicted. 

His campaign ad is all about not letting the wrong sort of people vote. He said, "The enemies of freedom don't get a vote in Ohio. Only you do." I am turned off by this patriotic speech that characterizes Trump's administration. It reminds me of 1984. 

He says he will share information from credit bureaus and financial services to update voter rolls, and will share voting information with the Department of Homeland Security. (I thought Republicans liked privacy. They require schools to be SOOOOOO careful with children's information online, but here Robert Sprague is blabbling financial details with federal agencies with dubious motives.)

Both candidates have concerns about elections. Sprague worries that bad people will vote. Russo worries that bad people will make it so all people cannot vote. 

I don't think immigrants are the bad guys. I think they are just as human as I am. I think they are willing to work harder than many Americans. I think our exclusionary policies will put us in a world of hurt in the future. As an educator, I love to teach their children and I will be tickled when they gain the skills to be well-paid professionals.

Also, Sprague's rhetoric is just giving fodder to Donald Trump to complain when he doesn't win. He may have already done his damage by suggesting there was so much wrong with our elections. No one ever cared about election security until Donald Trump lost an election. 

This is not a corruption vs. integrity race, I think. It seems like a partisan ideology race. Sprague is staying in line with Trump's election skepticism. Russo is making sure the vulnerable still have their voice heard (because they will vote Democrat, because Democrats will take care of them.) 

I just met Allison Russo on a video call. She said she is working to create a citizen-led map drawing process. Currently it is elected officials. The elected officials are all pretty much Republicans, and they drew the map to benefit Republicans in congress. 55% of Ohioans voted for Trump, but Republicans got 67% of congressional seats. The law in Ohio says that districts should be contiguous (touching, at least a little) and compact. 

Tell me which of these districts are compact.   
![Ohio District 36](/post-images/district-36.png). 
![Ohio District 38](/post-images/district-38.png). 
![Ohio District 40](/post-images/district-40.png). 

Cities usually vote Democrat, and rural areas usually vote Republican. There are many Democrats in dense cities. If you divide the districts like a salsa chip plate, with a circle in the middle, the districts are compact. All the Democrats in that compact city district in the center vote for the Democrat, and half the seats in Congress are Democrats, if half the voters are Democrats.  Republicans are more spread out in the country. If you slice up the city like a pizza, and give each slice of the countryside a little tip of the city, then the Republican vote can overwhelm the Democrat vote. None of the people changed their minds. Just the boundaries changed. Now there are fewer Democrats in Congress. 

This is an issue because it can suppress voter voices. It is hard to get a Democrat majority when the maps are drawn to favor Republicans. This is anti-democracy. 

Do I think that Robert Sprague will lift a finger to change the maps? I do not.

Allison Russo is running on the promise that she will create an independent committee to draw maps. I like this because the Congressional representatives can represent the people well. If like-minded voters are grouped together, representatives can focus on their needs. Right now, the needs of the billionaires are so well represented. But those who are struggling are not well represented. They will struggle more and more. The suffering will grow, and that is enough of a reason for me. But society will also become less productive, less beautiful, and less stable. The worse it gets, the harder it will be to fix. 

The first step is a fair map and fair elections. Allison Russo's entire platform are fair maps and elections.
