### Cassandra Rice
### vs David Brannon

Cassandra Rice is running against David Brannon because she says life in his courtroom is very unpleasant. He has two low reviews on Google, from people who usually give positive reviews. 

Rice made a post on Facebook detailing a refugee situation he presided over. A grandmother couldn't legally adopt her two African war-orphaned grandchildren because she couldn't pay the $72 fee. Brannon is scheduled to pay $300,000 for ads for his campaign, but refused justice to a refugee grandmother over $72. He turned away the fatherless, the widowed, and the stranger at his door in one fell swoop. This is not the judge that I would wish to come across in the legal system. I would not wish it on my children, or my neighbors, or anyone in my community. 

His other reviews says he's very intelligent. In an age of AI, heart is going to matter more than head, and I trust Cassandra Rice's heart more.
