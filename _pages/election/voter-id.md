I have always been asked for my photo ID. Ohio has very secure elections. Some people in government just want to reduce trust in elections so they have ground to complain when they lose. Republicans are usually in favor of small government, except when it comes to excluding the Democrat voter base. 

Obtaining photo ID does have a cost in some circumstances. When do you stop calling it a normal cost and start calling it a poll tax? If your voters are the ones paying it and not the other party's. 

This law doesn't change anything. Vote however you want; it won't make our elections more secure. Removing the politician who has already incited insurrection will make them more secure.
