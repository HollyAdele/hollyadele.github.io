### Marilyn Zayas
### vs. Daniel Hawkins

Marilyn Zayas' website says she grew up in "Crack City, USA" to a mother who emigrated from Puerto Rico. Her mother never finished eighth grade, but Zayas became a lawyer and a judge. I heard her speak last week. She said she has spent a lot of time talking to people about the root of the drug problem all over the state. She has a plan to help drug overdoses in rural communities.

Daniel Hawkins already sits on the Ohio Supreme Court (he is the incumbent). His bio includes starting a program working on blighted properties and assigning convicts to pick up trash. 

Blighted properties and drug overdose deaths are both problems in Ohio. I feel that the drug problem is a root cause of the blighten properties problem, though. I plan to vote Zayas.
