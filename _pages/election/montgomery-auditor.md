### Tammy Murphy
### vs. Karl Keith

Tammy Murphy is running on a platform of Property Taxes are Too High. Property taxes are high because remote work allowed people to move out of dense, expensive areas (like California) into less dense, less experience areas (like Montgomerty County). Since there are more people who want to live in Montgomery County, bidding wars for properties last longer and go higher. 

This is unfortunate for people who already live here, because they did nothing different but suddenly they have to pay more money to their mortgage lender so the mortgage lender can pay more taxes. 

Tammy Murphy wants to educate citizens about the ways they can get out of these taxes. I Googled "what do I do if I can't pay my property taxes?" and it told me where to go to apply for assistance. 

Out of 88 counties in Ohio, Karl Keith has was named, by other auditors, the "County Auditor of the Year" in 2012. He also received the "Lifetime Achievement Award" from the Ohio Government Finances Officers Association. 

Should we vote him out just so someone can tell people the same information they could learn from one Google search?

This is the same affordability argument that is being tossed around this election. We need to make a distinction about where the money is coming from and what the money is going to. 

Tariffs: going to Trump's pocket. 
More expensive items: Same amount going to businesses, additional going to tariffs, going to Trump.  
High gas prices: going to oil and gas companies and oil rich countries. 
High food prices: going to oil and gas companies and oil rich countries. 
Property taxes: going to our neighbors in need. 
 
It was not Karl Keith's fault that our properties went up in value. Voting out the incumbent because of market forces he did not create is not fair. Yesterday he was in Englewood helping homeowners sign up for the Homestead Exemption tax reduction. Today he went to the Senior Resource Fair at the Dayton Metro Library Northwest Branch. I saw a Facebook comment from a previous employee. She said she worked for him for 20 years and he was always fair. 

I think Karl Keith will be fine. He has worked as auditor for 25 years. He's not one of those politicians who hop from position to position, trying to climb the ladder. He seems like an honest guy who is doing a good job.
