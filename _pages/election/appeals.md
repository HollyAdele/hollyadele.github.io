### Chris Epley

This Republican has no challenger, so vote for him if you want. 

He is a member of the Oakwood Rotary Club. He was elected to the Oakwood Board of Education, appointed to the Oakwood City Council and elected two more times. He says that he is Experienced. Respected. Fair. 

Citizens of Oakwood would likely agree.
