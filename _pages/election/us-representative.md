I heard Kristina Knicerbocker speak a few weeks ago, and she is great. She was articulate, and she was passionate about helping people. She wants to ban congressional stock trading and prohibit congresspeople from becoming lobbyists. She wants to make sure data centers are paid for by their companies, not the public. She wants to repeal tariffs, build more affordable housing, work toward universal healthcare, protect the VA, grow Wright-Patt.
