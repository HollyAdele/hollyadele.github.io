### Richard Glennon
### vs. Steve Abshire

Steve Abshire has more experience and more relevant degrees. (Glennon majored in English and Humanities, Abshire in Applied Behavior Science and Urban Affairs.) Abshire has military experience. He's already been a magistrate (which is like a judge but I think is underneath a judge).

He says he's Tough. Compassionate. I wonder how he reconciles the two. He said he joined the army because he couldn't afford college. He was the first in his family to go to college. He worked as a CNC operater to put himself through college. He is affiliated with the Shriners and the Masons, which feel a little spooky to me. It's strange that he's endorsed by a labor union, because usually labor unions endorse Democrats. There's a picture of him on Facebook after a day of volunteering cleaning out other peoples' houses. 

I heard Richard Glennon speak. He didn't say why he was better than his opponent, he rambled, and had to be cut off by the moderator. I think he would be a just fine judge, but at this moment I think Abshire has more life experience and judicial experience.
