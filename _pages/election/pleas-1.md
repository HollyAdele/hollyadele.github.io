### Derek Muncy
### vs Anne Keeton

Derek Muncy has barely completed enough years as a lawyer to be allowed to take the office of judge. If the election had happened a few months earlier, he would not have been qualified. On Facebook, he said he spends most of his time chopping wood. He posted a picture of him with Charlie Kirk near a banner that says Big Government Sucks. (I think big government prevents Cyclospora and Ebola outbreaks, rebuilds after natural disasters, upholds the rule of law, brings prosperity to more people.)

Anne Keeton has been practicing law for 23 years. She was president of the Dayton Bar Association. She believes that no one is above the law. She reiterates that she treats people impartially and equal before the law. I have heard her speak and she is good. I have also seen her taking time to be at high school concerts and other community events. I want to say hi, but I have nothing to say. Hi, I think you're great, I'm going to vote for you?
